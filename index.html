Dr. Shallu Choudhary

I study the native bumble bees of the North-Western Himalayas: how they nest, what they forage on, the pathogens they carry, and how they can be reared to pollinate crops. My work sits where pollinator ecology, bee health and practical agriculture meet.

Research

Pollinator ecology

My doctoral research at Sher-e-Kashmir University of Agricultural Sciences and Technology of Jammu examines nesting behaviour and pathogen spillover in native bumble bees of the North-Western Himalayas of the Jammu region, including infection by Crithidia and Nosema. The aim is to understand these wild pollinators well enough to rear and domesticate them for crop pollination, without spreading disease to wild populations.

Before that, my M.Sc. thesis described the nest architecture and foraging behaviour of the small carpenter bee Ceratina viridissima, work that received the Mahima Best Thesis Award in 2022.
